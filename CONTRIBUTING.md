# Contributing to DAWSON

Thanks for your interest in improving the kingdom. Issues, bug reports,
and discussion are always welcome. Code contributions are welcome too,
under the terms below — please read them before opening a pull request.

project as a whole is sustained through commercial offerings (official
apps, hosted services, and commercial licenses). To keep that model
possible, the project requires that copyright in the codebase remain
consolidated with the maintainer.

By submitting a contribution (pull request, patch, or code snippet
intended for inclusion), you agree that:

1. **You wrote it, or have the right to submit it.** Your contribution
   is your original work, or you have sufficient rights to submit it
   under these terms, per the Developer Certificate of Origin
   (https://developercertificate.org). Sign off each commit with
   `git commit -s`, which adds the `Signed-off-by:` line.

2. **You grant the maintainer broad rights.** You grant Owen Ethan
   Brown a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable,
   royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, sublicense, and
   distribute your contribution, including the right to distribute it
   under licenses other than AGPL-3.0 (for example, in commercial
   builds or under future license versions).

3. **You keep your copyright.** This is a license, not an assignment.
   You remain the author of your contribution and may reuse your own
   work however you wish.

If you're not comfortable with these terms, that's okay — filing
detailed issues and testing releases are equally valuable ways to
contribute.

## Practical guidelines

- Open an issue describing the change before starting significant work,
  so effort isn't wasted on directions the project won't take.
- Match the existing code style and structure of the area you're
  touching.
- Keep pull requests focused: one change per PR.
